About us

A new firm with decades of experience.

Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys (MC Attorneys) is a registered law firm in terms of the Legal Practitioners Act, formed in April 2025.

Our story

Formed to deliver excellent legal services.

Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys is a modern law firm registered with the Law society of Zimbabwe formed in April 2025. We believe that exceptional legal service begins with understanding people, protecting their interests and finding practical solutions to complex challenges.

Built on a foundation of integrity, professionalism and collaboration, our firm brings together legal expertise and a forward-thinking approach to serving individuals, businesses and institutions. We understand that every legal matter carries its own challenges and opportunities, which is why we approach each instruction with care, precision and a clear focus on our clients' objectives.

Our practice is guided by a commitment to excellence, strategic thinking and the highest standards of professional conduct. We combine a deep respect for the law with a modern, solutions-driven mindset, enabling us to navigate complex legal issues while building lasting relationships founded on trust.

At the heart of our firm is the belief that the law should be more than a profession; it should be a means of protecting rights, creating opportunities and making a meaningful difference.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains unchanged: to deliver thoughtful legal solutions, uphold the principles of justice and provide representation our clients can trust.
