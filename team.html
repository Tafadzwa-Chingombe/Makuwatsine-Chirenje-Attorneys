Our team

Founding & Managing Partner

Tendai Makuwatsine

LLB (Hons), University of South Africa (UNISA)

Tendai Makuwatsine is the Founding and Managing Partner of Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys. He obtained his Bachelor of Laws (Honours) degree from the University of South Africa in 2016 and has practised law at several established law firms before co-founding Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys with Liberty Chirenje in 2025.

Tendai's practice has a strong focus on conveyancing and property law, with experience in property transfers, mortgage bond registrations and other Deeds Office transactions. He has also been involved in title creation for land development projects across Zimbabwe, providing legal support in property-related transactions and development matters.

His broader areas of practice include notarial practice, deceased estates administration, mining law, labour law, insolvency law, debt collection, commercial law, family law, civil litigation and intellectual property.

As Managing Partner, Tendai is committed to maintaining high standards of professionalism, integrity and client service. He approaches legal matters with diligence and a practical understanding of the legal and commercial considerations affecting his clients.

Outside his legal practice, Tendai is an avid golfer.

Founding and Senior Partner

Liberty Chirenje

LLB (Hons), University of Zimbabwe (UZ)

Liberty Chirenje is a Founding and Senior Partner of Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys, bringing a wealth of legal experience and a broad-based understanding of litigation and legal practice. He obtained his Bachelor of Laws (Honours) degree from the University of Zimbabwe in 2015 and was admitted to practise law on 17 February 2016.

Liberty began his legal career at Makuku Law Firm in February 2016, where he served as an Associate until December 2022. During this period, he played a key role in developing the firm's criminal law practice, handling criminal matters, including police trials. His work provided him with extensive practical experience in criminal litigation, while also allowing him to develop experience in conveyancing and notarial practice.

Over time, Liberty broadened his practice to include civil litigation, deceased estates, mining law and commercial law. In 2023, he joined Macharaga Law Chambers as an Associate, where he continued to develop his professional experience until April 2025.

In April 2025, Liberty joined forces with Tendai Makuwatsine to establish Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys. As a Founding Partner, he contributes to the firm's strategic direction and its commitment to providing professional, practical and effective legal services.

His areas of practice include criminal litigation, civil litigation, conveyancing, notarial practice and commercial law. Liberty approaches his work with diligence, professionalism and a commitment to protecting his clients' interests.
