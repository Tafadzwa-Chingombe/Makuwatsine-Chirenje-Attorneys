Our team

Lawyers from diverse backgrounds, one shared standard.

A managing partner, a founding partner and two senior associates, supported by professional assistants and dedicated staff.

Founding and Managing Partner

Tendai Makuwatsine

LLB (Hons) SA

He is the founding and managing partner of Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys. He attained his Bachelor of Laws (Hons) Degree from University of South Africa in 2016. Tendai has practiced law in a number of well-established law firms before founding this firm with Liberty Chirenje in 2025.

His specialty is in Conveyancing, he has extensive experience in Conveyancing matters such as registration of transfers and mortgage bonds with the Deeds Office. He is widely involved creating titles in land development and other conveyancing matters.

He is also a registered Legal Practitioners and Notary Public, his area of practice is Notarial Practice, administration of estates, mining law, labour law insolvency law, debt collection, commercial law, family law, general civil litigation and Intellectual property.

In his spare time he is an avid golfer.

Founding Partner

Liberty Chirenje

LLB (Hons) UZ, Zimbabwe

His introduction to the legal fraternity began with working for Makuku Law Firm in 2016 as an Associate. He started studying Law with University of Zimbabwe in 2010 and graduated in 2015 and admitted to practice on the 17th of February 2016.

He joined Makuku Law Firm in February 2016 to December 2022 as an Associate. Wherein he was tasked with making the criminal department visible. ln this regard he was solely responsible for handling criminal matters including police trials. During this period he acquired vast knowledge in criminal litigation and also did some notarial and conveyancing work and a notary public and conveyancer. He later on started to grew interest in other areas of practice and started to do civil litigation, Administration of Estates, mining law and commercial law.

In 2023 he joined Macharaga Law Chambers as an associate to until 1st April 2025 when he joined forces with Tendai Makuwatsine and became a Founding Partner of Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys. his area of practice includes Criminal, Conveyancing, Notarial Practice, Civil litigation and Commercial law.
