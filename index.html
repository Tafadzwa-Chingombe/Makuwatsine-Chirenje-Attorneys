FAQ

Questions about our firm

Who is Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys?

Makuwatsine Chirenje Attorneys (MC Attorneys) is a modern law firm in Harare, Zimbabwe, registered with the Law Society of Zimbabwe and formed in April 2025 by Tendai Makuwatsine and Liberty Chirenje. The firm serves individuals, businesses and institutions.

Where is the firm located?

The office is at 6 Londonderry Avenue, Eastlea, Harare, Zimbabwe. The landline is +263 24 2720094 and the email address is admin@makuwatsinechirenjeattorneys.co.zw.

What areas of law does the firm practise?

The firm practises Conveyancing and Notarial Practice, Criminal Law, Energy and Mining Law, Estate Planning and Administration of Estates, General Civil Litigation, Commercial and Corporate Law, Insurance Law, Intellectual Property, Labour Law, Family Law, Business Rescue and Insolvency.

How do I book a consultation?

Use the contact form on the Contact page, call +263 24 2720094, or email admin@makuwatsinechirenjeattorneys.co.zw. Tell us briefly about your matter and the right lawyer will respond.
